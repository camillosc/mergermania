MergerAnalyst Merger-Arb Model Portfolio

A paper portfolio of signed takeover deals tracked on MergerAnalyst. It buys targets trading below the agreed deal price, hedges stock-for-stock deals, and sizes each position by its estimated loss if the deal breaks.
